# Detailed Class Description: ExpressionEvaluator

#### 1. General Information
- **Class Name:** `ExpressionEvaluator`
- **Type:** Standard Utility Class (Calculator Engine)
- **Role:** Evaluates simple math expressions (`+`, `-`, `*`) in any selected system, and formats both the expression and result across all 6 systems.
- **Methods:** `evaluate(expressionInput, system)` parses operands, performs math calculation, checks bounds [1..3999], and constructs `CalculatorResult`.

#### 8. Simplest Terms
**Real-World Analogy:** A math teacher who accepts an equation in any language, solves it, and presents the problem and answer in 6 languages.
